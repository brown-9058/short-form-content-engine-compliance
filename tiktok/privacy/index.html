TikTok Integration Privacy Policy

Effective / updated: October 11, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to TikTok features used by Short Form Content Engine, a privately operated local desktop creator workflow. It is separate from the YouTube/API Privacy Policy. This website is informational and does not operate a hosted account or upload service.

Data received from TikTok

Authorized use may provide a TikTok account identifier (open ID), authorization scopes, access and refresh credentials and their expiry metadata, upload/publish identifiers, and upload processing/status information.

The configured user.info.basic permission can provide basic account/profile information, such as display name and avatar, when authorized and retrieved. The current local implementation uses the account identifier returned during authorization and does not yet retrieve basic-profile information.

The integration does not request access to private messages, contacts, followers, analytics, email, phone numbers or a library of videos not selected for this integration.

Creator-provided data

Local records may include the video selected for upload, caption and attribution suggestions, account/channel alias, approved artifact references and an explicit human confirmation of posting. Captions and metadata are prepared locally for the creator; draft upload does not automatically apply them in TikTok.

Purpose of processing

  • Authenticate an authorized creator and identify the intended TikTok account.
  • Deliver a creator-approved video as a TikTok draft.
  • Prevent accidental duplicate uploads.
  • Reconcile upload and processing status.
  • Maintain local audit and history records.

Data is used to operate this integration, rather than for advertising profiling.

Token security

TikTok OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally. macOS Keychain is preferred; a permission-restricted local filesystem fallback may be used when explicitly configured. Tokens are not committed to the public repository. Secrets are not intentionally written to normal application logs.

Sharing

TikTok integration data is not sold. TikTok receives the selected video and authenticated requests necessary to deliver and process a draft. Local records are available to the creator and people they explicitly authorize to access their computer or workflow. This local integration does not introduce a cloud processing service.

TikTok handles information it receives under its own Privacy Policy. This static website has no tracking scripts, advertising, forms, login, embedded players or application cookies. GitHub hosts the site and may process ordinary hosting requests under its Privacy Statement.

Retention

Local authorization and upload records are retained as needed to operate the integration, preserve duplicate-upload protection and maintain audit history. No fixed TikTok retention period has been formally defined. Records may be removed when no longer required. Removing upload history can remove duplicate-protection evidence and requires care before further transfers. Deleting local data does not delete content already delivered to TikTok.

Revocation and deletion

A creator can revoke authorization through TikTok's account permission controls and remove locally stored TikTok credentials and records. Local removal alone does not revoke an active TikTok grant; revocation alone does not erase existing local records. Contact the project operator for help with local removal. There is no web account-deletion feature. Drafts and posted content must be managed separately inside TikTok.

Security

The workflow uses official API connections, local credential safeguards, checks of approved artifacts and redacted diagnostics. Creators should protect their device and access to local records. No security measure can guarantee that data will never be accessed improperly.

Children's privacy

The integration is not designed to knowingly collect data from children through this developer integration. Creators must meet TikTok's applicable account and authorization requirements. Contact the operator if you believe data was collected inappropriately.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the integration changes. The effective/update date is shown above. Material changes to collection or use will be described here before representing them as current functionality.
