Short Form Content Engine — TikTok Integration

Desktop app for preparing short educational videos and sending approved videos to TikTok as drafts for final review.

Overview

Short Form Content Engine is a desktop application for preparing and managing short-form educational videos. The TikTok integration is designed to allow an authorized creator to connect their TikTok account and send an approved video to TikTok as a draft.

This integration is privately operated and under development. It is not generally available, and this site does not claim TikTok production approval or certification. Availability depends on TikTok permissions, app review and account eligibility.

How it works

  1. The creator authorizes their TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit.
  2. Short Form Content Engine confirms the connected account with the creator.
  3. The creator selects an internally reviewed and approved video.
  4. The application validates the exact approved video artifact.
  5. The application uses TikTok's Content Posting API video.upload capability to upload the video as a draft.
  6. TikTok delivers the draft to the creator's inbox for completion.
  7. The creator completes final review, edits, disclosures and publication inside TikTok.

Short Form Content Engine does not use TikTok Direct Post and does not automatically publish videos to a creator's public TikTok profile.

Draft delivery is separate from publication. A delivery or processing receipt does not establish that a video is publicly visible.

TikTok permissions

The Developer Portal configuration specifies Login Kit and Content Posting API for Desktop, with these two permissions:

user.info.basic

Used to identify and confirm the TikTok account that the creator authorizes, with basic account/profile information when granted and retrieved. The current local draft-upload implementation confirms the account identifier returned during authorization; basic-profile retrieval is not yet implemented.

video.upload

Used to send a creator-selected, approved video to TikTok as a draft. The current local upload flow requests this permission. Direct Post is off.

Creator control

Authorization is initiated by the creator. Two creator identities may be connected independently, with separate local account bindings; connecting one does not authorize the other.

Creators choose which approved video to send. Drafts still require the creator to complete TikTok's posting flow, including captions, rights credits, disclosures and settings. Local caption and attribution suggestions support that review; they are not automatically applied by the draft-upload API.

Revoking authorization through TikTok stops future authorized access. Removing local credentials disconnects that local installation; it does not itself revoke a grant or delete an already delivered draft or published video.

Data handling

Authorization credentials are stored locally, preferably in macOS Keychain, with an explicitly configured secure local-file fallback. Account identifiers, upload status and local audit records support correct routing and duplicate-upload protection. This website does not collect login credentials or host the upload workflow.
