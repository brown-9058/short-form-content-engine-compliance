Effective / updated: October 11, 2026

Scope

These terms govern use of TikTok integration features in Short Form Content Engine, a privately operated desktop workflow. They do not offer public access, subscriptions or a hosted service. The YouTube/API Terms of Use separately govern the YouTube integration.

Authorized accounts

Creators must connect only TikTok accounts they are authorized to use. Each connected identity must be confirmed independently. Do not use another person's authorization or credentials without permission.

Creator responsibility

The creator remains responsible for the content selected, its accuracy and legality, all required rights/licenses and attribution, and compliance with TikTok Community Guidelines, TikTok Terms of Service and applicable law.

The creator must complete final TikTok review, caption edits, disclosures and settings, including applicable AI-generated-content labeling. Internal approval and technical checks do not replace these responsibilities or guarantee that TikTok will accept the content.

Draft upload behavior

The integration sends a selected, approved video as a TikTok inbox draft using Content Posting API. It does not guarantee public publication. Direct Post is not used. Final publication occurs through TikTok and remains under creator control. Local completion records are human assertions unless separately confirmed by platform status; they do not independently prove public visibility.

Availability

Authorization, draft delivery and processing may be unavailable because of TikTok API availability, expired or revoked authorization, account restrictions, rate or usage limits, app approval requirements, or API and policy changes. Uptime, draft delivery and publication are not guaranteed.

Prohibited use

  • Abuse of the API, fraudulent activity or deceptive use.

TikTok is an independent third-party service. Its own terms and policies apply independently to authorization, draft handling and posting. Short Form Content Engine is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by TikTok. No TikTok approval or certification is implied by this website.
